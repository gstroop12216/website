Dress for the moment

Uniform & meeting expectations

Please make sure your Daisy is dressed for meetings and formal events. Our troop requires that each Scout wear the official vest/sash at all meetings and outings unless otherwise stated. For outings, her vest/tunic is to be worn with a white polo shirt, button down shirt or a Girl Scout themed t-shirt and khaki pants, shorts, skort or level color tights.

Closed toed shoes are required at all meetings and activities. Each girl should be neat in appearance and keep her uniform in good condition.
