Troop members

Everything families need to know

Useful information for joining or continuing with our troop.

01

New Girl Registration

If you have not registered your daughter with Girl Scout USA, you can do it online here and let us know when she is signed up so that we can have her transferred to our troop.

Registration is $45 with GSUSA + $15 with GSSEF. Registering online is optional; you are more than welcome to register her in person at our meetings. Please check with the troop leader for openings.

02

Returning Girls

Please reregister your daughter by logging into your mygs.girlsouts.org account. Let us know if you have any issues reregistering.

03

Financial Assistance

Financial assistance is available if needed. Please click here for more information.

04

Girls being transferred

If your daughter is being transferred to our troop, please email us her old troop #, her full name and her date of birth.

05

Parent Volunteers

From time to time, we will need volunteers to participate in meetings and chaperone events (cookie booths, trips, etc.). All parents/family members MUST be a registered volunteer to participate in any event with our troop — no exceptions; this is not only for the safety of our girls, but also for liability purposes.

Volunteer registration costs $30 for the year. Click here for the application which includes a background check form. Please execute it completely and bring to our first meeting with your payment.
