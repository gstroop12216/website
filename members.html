Membership

Registration

New

If you have not registered your daughter with Girl Scout USA, you can do it online here. Let us know when she is signed up so that we can have her transferred to our troop.

Returning

Please reregister your daughter by logging into your mygs.girlscouts.org account. Let us know if you have any issues reregistering.

Transferring

If your daughter is being transferred to our troop, please email us her old troop number, her full name, and her date of birth.

Troop costs

Fees

Registration Fees

Annual registration is $45 with GSUSA plus $15 with GSSEF.

Registering online is optional; you are welcome to register in person at our meetings. Please check with the troop leader for openings.

Troop Dues

Dues help us provide meeting/party supplies, badges and much more. This is a separate fee from your annual membership to council.

Dues are $65 and you can send it through Zelle to gstroop12216@gmail.com.

Financial assistance: Financial assistance is available if needed. Please click here for more information.

Get involved

Parent Volunteers

From time to time, we will need volunteers to participate in meetings and chaperone events (cookie booths, trips, etc.). All parents/family members MUST be a registered volunteer to participate in any event with our troop — no exceptions; this is not only for the safety of our girls, but also for liability purposes.

Volunteer registration costs $30 for the year. Click here for the application, which includes a background check form. Please complete it and bring it to our first meeting with your payment.
